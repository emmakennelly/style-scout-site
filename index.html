Style Scout

A personal tool that learns my fashion style from images I've saved and finds similar clothing at online stores I like.

What it does

  • Reads my own saved style images, including my Pinterest style boards, with read-only access.
  • Learns the looks I keep coming back to, such as colors, fits and materials.
  • Checks new arrivals at my favorite stores and shortlists the closest matches for me to review.

Who uses it

Only me. Style Scout is a single-user tool that runs on my own computer. It has no other users and no sign-ups.

What it doesn't do

  • It doesn't post, pin, save or change anything on Pinterest.
  • It doesn't access anyone else's account.
  • It doesn't sell, share or publish data.
